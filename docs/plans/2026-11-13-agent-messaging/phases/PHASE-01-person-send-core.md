- Goal: A signed-in person's request to a chat agent is stored, queued as that person's own message with its provenance (waiting behind a pending approval, a pending question and a held or errored queue), retried without duplicates, followed through queued, waiting, read, answered, not answered and cancelled, its answer kept independent of the target, and refused for every agent credential, with server tests, contract schemas and regenerated fixtures.
- Round: 1
- Depends on: none
- Owns: `apps/server/src/send_requests/**`; `apps/server/src/persistence/migrations.rs` (migration 75 and its assertion lists); `apps/server/src/persistence/repositories.rs` (the `person_send` message field and `thread_blocks_new_turn`); `apps/server/src/orchestration/engine.rs` (person-send admission, the two agent predicates, projection); `apps/server/src/production/orchestration_rpc.rs` (public refusal, snapshot field, server dispatcher with `ServerActor`, the forced queue); `apps/server/src/production/runtime.rs` (service wiring); `apps/server/src/production/mcp_tools_tests/mod.rs` and `send_requests.rs` (harness helper); `apps/server/src/production/connect_mcp/tests.rs` (credential refusal tests); `apps/server/src/rpc/methods.rs`; `apps/server/src/auth/scope.rs`; `apps/server/src/lib.rs`; `apps/server/src/lifecycle.rs` (capability flag); the test-literal sites of `ProjectionThreadMessage` under `apps/server/tests/**`; `packages/contracts/src/sendRequests.ts` and its test, `rpc.ts`, `orchestration.ts`, `orchestration.test.ts`, `environment.ts`, `environment.test.ts`, `index.ts`, `packages/contracts/fixtures/**`; `packages/shared/src/testSupport.ts`; `packages/client-runtime/src/state/threadReducer.ts` and its test.
- Must not touch: `apps/web/**`; `apps/server/src/agent_messaging/**`, `apps/server/src/mcp/**` and `apps/server/src/production/turn_delivery.rs` (phase 5); `apps/server/src/provider_terminal/**`; `apps/server/src/terminal/**`.
